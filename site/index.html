Can you tell?

Each round shows two snapshots of the same scene. In one the robot succeeded, in the other it failed by a few millimeters. Pick the success. Then see the ground truth, what the vision models said, and what three cheap sensors read.

Leaderboard

Sensor classifier under increasing noise

Logistic regression on degraded sensor signals. Strength 1 is the default cheap-hardware model (noise, 8 to 10 bit quantization, drift, latency, dropouts). The dashed line is the best vision model on the same test set.

Minimal pairs

Same scene, same lighting, same camera. Left succeeded, right failed. Pixel difference is the mean absolute change per pixel between the two front images (0 to 255).

Millimeters to fool

For each model and task, the attacker searches for the largest failure, in millimeters, that the model still calls a success. Ground truth is re-checked in simulation for every candidate. Higher bars mean easier to fool. Zero means the model was never fooled on the search ladder.

The exact simulator state at the moment both judges were asked. Drag to orbit. Switch between the success and failure of each pair.
